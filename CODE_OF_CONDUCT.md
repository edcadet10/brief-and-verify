# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Brief & Verify is a public learning project. We are committed to a respectful, accessible, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, ability, appearance, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and changes without attacking people;
- give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in good faith;
- make room for newcomers and explain project conventions when useful;
- respect privacy, consent, attribution, and accessibility needs;
- avoid sharing real personal, health, student, customer, case, or confidential data; and
- step back when a maintainer asks for a conversation to pause or move.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, repeated disruption, and knowingly unsafe or deceptive contributions are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, review threads, and public spaces where someone is clearly representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Depending on severity and pattern, a response may include a clarification, formal warning, temporary participation limit, or permanent ban. Enforcement decisions will prioritize safety, context, proportionality, and the privacy of affected people.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports or identifying details in an issue or Discussion.

- For abusive behavior on GitHub, use the **Report abuse or spam** option on the relevant content or account. GitHub documents that process in its [abuse-reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
- For a non-sensitive, project-specific moderation concern that can be discussed publicly, open a [Discussion](https://github.com/edcadet10/brief-and-verify/discussions) and label the concern clearly without including private information.

This project does not currently publish a separate private maintainer mailbox for conduct reports. Confidential platform conduct concerns should therefore use GitHub’s private reporting flow above; do not redirect them to the security vulnerability form.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation.
